Chess where the board is only the studio.

1 · Play — you are Ivory, the PC is Obsidian. Normal movement, but no checkmate: kings can be captured and nobody wins by trapping one.
2 · Shoot — each player gets 3 photos. Frame the board, set focal length, aperture, shutter and ISO.
3 · Score — exposure, sharpness, rule-of-thirds composition, piece value in frame and depth of field. Best total of 3 photos wins.
